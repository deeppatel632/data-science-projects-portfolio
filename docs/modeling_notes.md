# Machine Learning & Modeling Methodologies

## 1. Customer Purchase Intent Classification (Project 1)

### Target Leakage Prevention: The PageValues Dilemma
In web analytics systems (e.g. Google Analytics), `PageValues` is computed by dividing the total revenue generated by the number of unique page views prior to the transaction.
- **Why It Causes Leakage:** For an ongoing uncompleted session in real time, `PageValues` cannot be known until the transaction actually occurs. Including it in predictive features produces an unrealistically inflated ROC-AUC (>0.93) that completely fails when deployed on live incoming traffic.
- **Engineering Fix:** By default, `PageValues` is strictly excluded from our feature matrix. An interactive educational toggle allows users to observe the artificial performance jump firsthand.

### Model Formulation & Class Imbalance
- **Baseline Models:**
  - **Logistic Regression:** Regularized linear classifier serving as an interpretable probabilistic baseline.
  - **Random Forest:** Ensemble of 120 decision trees (max depth 10) evaluating non-linear feature interactions.
- **Handling Class Imbalance:** With only ~15.5% positive conversion sessions, both models configure `class_weight='balanced'`.
- **Threshold Tuning:** The interactive decision slider allows operators to adjust the probability cutoff ($0.10$ to $0.90$) to balance high-recall sales interventions against false alarm costs.

## 2. Student Grade Regression (Project 2)

### Modeling Honesty: Two-Scenario Operational Design
The UCI Student dataset includes interim exam marks $G1$ and $G2$. Because academic grading is cumulative, $G1$ and $G2$ have an overwhelming correlation with $G3$ ($r \approx 0.85$).
1. **Early-Support Scenario (Excluding G1 & G2):**
   - Purpose: Matriculation risk assessment before any exams occur.
   - Realistic Performance: $R^2 \approx 0.15 - 0.25$, $RMSE \approx 4.0$ grades.
   - Insights: Captures weak signals from past class failures, mother's education, and study time.
2. **Previous-Grades Scenario (Including G1 & G2):**
   - Purpose: Mid-term grade trajectory refinement.
   - Performance: $R^2 \approx 0.75 - 0.85$, $RMSE \approx 1.5 - 2.0$ grades.
   - Insights: Interim exams dominate all other features, reducing background covariates to marginal status.

## 3. Commercial Demand Forecasting (Project 3)

### Chronological Moving-Average Baseline
Rather than applying an opaque deep neural network to daily sales data, the restaurant project implements an interpretable chronological baseline:
- **Rolling Window:** 7-day moving average capturing weekly cyclicality.
- **Trend Extrapolation:** Trailing linear slope projecting the forward 14 days.
- **Evaluation Discipline:** Time series observations are evaluated in strict chronological order without temporal shuffling.
- **Data Sufficiency Guard:** Requires at least 14 days of historical daily observations before allowing forward projections.
